Setup steps (per phone)

The phone must be factory-reset with no Google account added. If setup has already started, reset again before continuing.
  1. Factory reset the phone (Settings → System → Reset → Erase all data). A brand-new phone out of the box is already in this state.
  2. Power on and stop at the Welcome screen. Do not tap Start or add a Google account.
  3. Tap the screen 6 times in the same spot (center of the screen). This opens the QR scanner.
  4. Grant camera permission when prompted, then scan the QR code above. Hold the phone 20–30 cm away in good light.
  5. The phone will automatically:
    • Join the configured Wi-Fi
    • Download and install the APK
    • Set the app as Device Owner
    This takes 1–2 minutes. Keep the phone plugged in or above 30% battery.
  6. Setup completes. The phone reaches the home screen. The app is accessible from Settings → Apps.
  7. Open the app from Settings → Apps → LNVS Internal and sign in with the admin code. Grant all requested permissions:
    • Notifications
    • Call log
    • Notification access (toggle on in Settings)
    • Unrestricted battery
The app does not appear in the launcher. It is only visible under Settings → Apps. Uninstall is blocked while the app is Device Owner, until unlocked from the in-app menu.
